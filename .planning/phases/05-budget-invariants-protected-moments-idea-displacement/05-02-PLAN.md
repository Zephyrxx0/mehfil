<threat_model>
## Threat Model Mitigation
- **T-05-05 (Timezone Day-Shift Desynchronization):** Evaluating dates via `new Date(string)` in browser local time leads to day shifts (-1 day in Western zones or +1 day in Eastern zones). *Mitigation:* `detectEnvironmentalRisks` and `calculateBackwardsTimeline` use pure UTC string parsing and UTC Date arithmetic exclusively.
- **T-05-06 (Alarmist Organizer Panic in Compressed Mode):** Events planned on short notice (<60 days) display demoralizing red error banners or 25 overdue tasks. *Mitigation:* Calm Compressed Mode design (ADR-0005) presents an affirming warm Marigold/Plum banner and isolates an immediate critical path triage sprint without overdue warnings.
- **T-05-07 (Unbounded Floating Idea Costs):** Curated ideas introduce floating currency values or unverified costs. *Mitigation:* All costs in `CURATED_IDEAS` are hardcoded whole-integer `Paise` checked in unit test assertions.
</threat_model>
