## Typography

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 |
| Label | 14px | 500 | 1.4 |
| Heading | 36px | 700 | 1.15 |

Additional Typographic Rules:
- **Hero Display:** `Fraunces` variable soft serif, size `clamp(3.5rem, 8vw, 6.75rem)`, weight 700–900, line-height 1.05, tracking `-0.03em`.
- **Wordmark & Devanagari:** `Tiro Devanagari Hindi` for "महफ़िल" brand wordmark and ethereal background mandala aura.
- **Section Headings (H1):** `Fraunces`, `clamp(2.25rem, 5vw, 3.75rem)`, weight 700, line-height 1.15, tracking `-0.02em`.
- **Card Titles & Subheads (H2):** `Fraunces`, `clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.25rem)`, weight 600, line-height 1.25, tracking `-0.01em`.
- **Category Titles (H3):** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 18px (1.125rem), weight 600, line-height 1.4.
- **Body Standard:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 16px (1.0rem), weight 400–500, line-height 1.5.
- **Captions & Metadata:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 14px (0.875rem), weight 500, line-height 1.4, tracking `+0.01em`.
- **Tabular Numerals & Currency:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (`tnum`), weight 600, line-height 1.2, formatted with Indian digit groupings (`₹5,00,000`).

## Color

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | #FFF8EE | Background surfaces, card bodies, modal/sheet surfaces, pill containers |
| Secondary (30%) | #F7C873 | Primary buttons, active slider track, milestone dots, progress indicators |
| Accent (10%) | #F2A7B8 | Protected Moment locks/badges, romantic/festive highlights, secondary micro-interactions |
| Destructive | #D95D39 | Calculation shortfall banners, budget overruns, infeasible state alerts |

Accent reserved for: Protected Moment locks/badges, romantic/festive highlights, and secondary micro-interactions (never generic buttons or text fills).

Neutral / Ink & Contrast Matrix:
- **Royal Plum (`#3A2442`):** Used for ALL typography, hairline borders (`border-royal-plum/10`), icons, and focus rings.
- **Strict WCAG AAA Standard:** White text on pastels is strictly forbidden. All typography rendered in Royal Plum.
  - Plum on Warm Ivory (`#FFF8EE`): Contrast ratio `13.18:1` (Exceeds WCAG AAA requirement of 7.0:1)
  - Plum on Marigold (`#F7C873`): Contrast ratio `8.91:1` (Exceeds WCAG AAA requirement of 7.0:1)
  - Plum on Gulabi Rose (`#F2A7B8`): Contrast ratio `7.28:1` (Exceeds WCAG AAA requirement of 7.0:1)
  - White on Marigold / Rose: Contrast `< 2.0:1` (Strictly prohibited by design and automated AST/regex tests)

Semantic Supporting Tints:
- **Hairline Borders:** `rgba(58, 36, 66, 0.08)` / `border-royal-plum/10`
- **Hover Overlays:** `rgba(58, 36, 66, 0.04)` (`plum/4`)
- **Muted Surface:** `#FBF2E3` (Ivory-900)
- **Verified / Success:** `#4A7C59` (Cardamom Green)
