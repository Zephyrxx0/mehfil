1. Create `src/components/hero/CelebrationParticles.tsx` (citing D-05, D-07, D-08):
   - Use `useReducedMotion()` from `motion/react`.
   - Maintain a pool of 25–35 particles with random initial positions (`x: [0, width]`, `y: [0, height]`), sizes (8px–16px), colors (Marigold `#F7C873` and Gulabi Rose `#F2A7B8` with 0.4–0.7 alpha), horizontal drift speeds, and angular velocities.
