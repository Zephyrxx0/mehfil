# Plan 03-02: Capability Router, Share & Save Modal & Celebratory Family Share View

<objective>
**As a** celebration host or invited family member,  
**I want to** seamlessly claim edit capability across devices via unguessable URL tokens (`/plan/[id]?key=[token]`) with instant URL cleaning and read-only downgrade, share the plan via a 2-tab Share & Save modal with phone-scannable QR codes and WhatsApp triggers, and provide family guests with a celebratory, server-sanitized editorial showcase (`/share/[token]`) featuring ceremony milestones and 1-click filtered guest calendar export,  
**so that** organizers can freely switch devices without passwords while family members admire the celebration countdown and ceremony dates with zero exposure of private vendor quotes, negotiation notes, or contingency buffers.
</objective>

   - Compares with `events.edit_key_hash` using `constantTimeCompare`.

   - If user has no edit cookie and no key parameter: sets `isReadOnly = true` and passes down to `PlanWorkspace`.
   - Top-bar renders `🔒 Read-Only View` badge with `Unlock Organizer Editing` button when `isReadOnly = true`.
