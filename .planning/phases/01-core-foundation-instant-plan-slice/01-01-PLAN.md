   - `EventType`: `'wedding' | 'birthday' | 'community'`
   - `LineType`: `'per_guest' | 'fixed' | 'flexible' | 'buffer'`
   - `BudgetCategory`: `'venue' | 'catering' | 'decor' | 'photo_film' | 'entertainment' | 'favors' | 'misc' | 'contingency'`
   - `BudgetLine`: `{ id: string; name: string; category: BudgetCategory; lineType: LineType; baselinePaise: number; currentPaise: number; elasticityRank?: number; isProtected?: boolean; }`
   - `EventBrief`: `{ title: string; eventType: EventType; eventDate: string; city: string; guestCount: number; totalBudgetPaise: number; protectedCategory: BudgetCategory; nuances?: string[]; customNotes?: string; }`
   - `Plan`: `{ id: string; brief: EventBrief; lines: BudgetLine[]; totalBudgetPaise: number; isInfeasible?: boolean; shortfallPaise?: number; remedies?: ShortfallRemedy[]; }`
   - `ShortfallRemedy`: `{ type: 'increase_budget' | 'reduce_guests' | 'trim_elastic'; title: string; description: string; deltaValue: number; }`
2. In `src/lib/engine/currency.ts`, implement:
   - `formatINR(paise: number, options?: { compact?: boolean }): string`:
     Formats paise into authentic Indian numbering system with commas (`₹25,00,000`, `₹5,00,000`, `₹1,50,00,000`) or compact lakh/crore badges (`₹25L`, `₹5L`, `₹1.5Cr`) per D-08.
   - `parseINRInput(input: string): number`:
     Converts user inputs like `"25L"`, `"25 Lakh"`, `"2.5Cr"`, `"2500000"`, `"₹25,00,000"` into integer paise ($1 \text{ Rupee} = 100 \text{ Paise}$).
3. In `src/lib/engine/archetypes.ts`, implement hardcoded archetypes (D-05, D-06, D-07):

     - `birthday`: Milestone 50th Birthday (80 guests, ₹5L baseline, 7 categories + 5% contingency buffer, default Protected Moment: `entertainment`).

   - `getArchetype(type: EventType): ArchetypeTemplate`
   - `instantiatePlanFromBrief(brief: EventBrief): Plan`:
     Calculates allocations using Hare-Niemeyer rupee boundary apportionment (D-10) and assigns the default or user-selected Protected Moment (D-07). Instantiation executes deterministically in <10ms without external dependencies (BRIEF-02, ADR-0001).
4. Create comprehensive tests in `tests/engine/currency.test.ts` and `tests/engine/archetypes.test.ts`.

<action>
1. In `src/lib/engine/budget.ts`, implement:
   - `apportionRupees(totalPaise: number, weights: { id: string; weight: number }[]): Record<string, number>`:
     Hare-Niemeyer largest-remainder algorithm operating at the whole Rupee boundary (D-10), guaranteeing:
     $$\sum \text{allocatedPaise} \equiv \text{totalPaise}$$
     without floating-point drift or ±₹1 display discrepancies.
   - `rebalanceGuestCount(plan: Plan, newGuestCount: number): Plan`:
     - Computes scaling factor $r = \text{newGuestCount} / \text{baselineGuestCount}$.
     - Per-guest lines (`catering`, `favors`) scale upwards proportionally with guest count.
     - Protected Moment line (`isProtected: true`) is **completely immune** to downward reductions (MOMENT-01, ADR-0004).
     - Elastic donor lines (`decor`, `entertainment`, `misc`) are reduced in elasticity rank order down to a strict **40% baseline floor** (D-11).
     - Fixed lines (`venue`, `photo_film`) and the 5% contingency buffer remain stable.
     - Invariant: total budget remains fixed (D-09, BUDGET-01).
   - `calculateShortfallRemedies(plan: Plan, deficitPaise: number): ShortfallRemedy[]`:
     Per ADR-0002, when demand exceeds donor capacity, flag `plan.isInfeasible = true` and compute 3 deterministic remedies:
     1. *Increase Budget*: add `deficitPaise` to total budget.
     2. *Reduce Guest Count*: compute maximum feasible guest count $G_{\max}$ that balances within donor capacity.
     3. *Trim Elastic Lines*: provide specific amounts to prune from flexible lines.
   - `setProtectedMoment(plan: Plan, category: BudgetCategory): Plan`:
     Reassigns the star/lock to the requested category (D-07), ensuring only one Protected Moment is active at a time and re-running rebalance if needed.
2. In `tests/engine/budget.test.ts`, write property-based and invariant tests:
   - 1,000 randomized total budgets (from ₹50,000 to ₹100 Crore) verifying exact sum equality $\sum \text{line.paise} \equiv \text{totalPaise}$.
   - Guest scale increase test: verify per-guest lines scale up, elastic lines trim, and total sum remains unchanged.
   - Floor constraint test: extreme guest scaling (10x) confirms elastic lines never drop below 40% of baseline (D-11).
   - Protected Moment immunity test: verify Protected Moment allocation never drops even under heavy guest scaling (MOMENT-01).
   - Shortfall test: verify ADR-0002 remedies provide exact mathematical answers.
</action>
